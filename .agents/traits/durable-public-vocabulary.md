name: durable-public-vocabulary
subject: Names, public documentation, UI copy, and rendered errors.
requirement: Names, public documentation, UI copy, and rendered errors use
  durable vocabulary appropriate to their audience, including the ordinary
  domain phrases readers are likely to search for. Ticket names, migration
  phases, internal storage fields, framework mechanics, and planning language
  stay in internal implementation or planning material.
purpose: Keeps public text meaningful after the project's internal history changes.
---
Prefer the terms the product's users and domain experts use for people, roles, and records, applied the same way everywhere.
